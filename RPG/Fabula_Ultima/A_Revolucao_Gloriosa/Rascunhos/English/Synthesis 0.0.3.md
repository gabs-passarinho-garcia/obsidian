# 🍎 The Weight of Truth: The Inverted Tower (Synthesis 0.0.3)

![[Torre_art.png]]

> *"Metal will yield. The weight will claim what is its own. The Law does not bend to our will, Robert."* — Isaac Newton

## 📜 Logline & Premise
A group of heroes arrives at Freefall Village, a place where the very fabric of reality is bleeding: sheep float aimlessly toward the clouds, and birds are brutally crushed against the cobblestones. The epicenter of this chaos is the **Inverted Tower**, a monument of black obsidian and twisted iron. To save the region, the party must "descend" up to the tower's peak to stop **Robert Hooke**, a broken architect attempting to destroy the laws of gravity to erase his guilt over the death of the woman he loved. 

What they don't know is that the expedition carries its own poison: old political grudges and a false ally willing to usurp the throne of the apocalypse.

---

## 📖 The Genesis of the Fall (Expanded Backstory)
The ruin of Woolsthorpe began when **Helena**, a brilliant, calloused blacksmith, bought two forbidden treasures from **Balthazar**, a chaotic smuggler: the *Manuscripts of Da Vinci* and the *Calculations of Galileo*. 

Around this time, **Isaac Newton** discarded his lover, the ambitious noblewoman **Isabella Stuart**, choosing the quiet perfection of mathematics over her loud political schemes. Isabella retreated to the shadows, brewing a cold hatred.

Newton found solace in Helena. But under an apple tree, a falling apple revealed the absolute Law of Gravity to him, while Helena chose to bite the fruit, embracing the freedom of flight. She gave Galileo's math to Newton and Da Vinci's dreams to Hooke. Hooke built the **Ascendant Engine**. Despite Newton's warnings that the metal would yield, Helena flew the machine. The core overheated, melting the structure and tearing her skin before the brutal fall. 

Hooke's mind shattered. He blamed Gravity—the Creator's Law—for killing his love. He built the Inverted Tower to wage war on Creation, imprisoning Newton inside it out of pure spite. Meanwhile, Isabella smiled in the shadows, waiting for the right group of heroes to clear her path to the Ascendant Engine.

## 🏘️ The Hub of Despair: Freefall Village
Before facing the Tower, the heroes must navigate the misery of those left behind. The village sets the visceral tone of the adventure:
* **The Weight of Resistance:** **Silas** (a shepherd buried in mud holding his floating sheep), **Thomas** (the sheriff wearing 40kg of armor as an anchor), and **Kael** (a bitter tavern keeper serving drinks in lead mugs).
* **The Fractured Minds:** **Elara** (Helena's traumatized apprentice, chained to an anvil) and **Rev. Samuel** (preaching from the ceiling of his inverted church).
* **The Opportunists:** **Balthazar** (the smuggler profiting from the chaos), **Zé da Corda** (charging "anchor tolls" in the streets), and **Caramelo** (a stray dog that inexplicably ignores gravity altogether).

## 🎭 The Fractured Quartet (Core Characters)

### ⚙️ Robert Hooke: The Architect of Chaos
![[Hooke_art.png]]
Driven mad by guilt, [[Hooke]] is a physical and theological terrorist. He wears an aristocratic coat stained with grease and Helena's dried blood. He blames the "oppressive weight" of the universe for her death.

### 🍎 Isaac Newton: The Prisoner of Law
![[Newton_art.png]]
Imprisoned in the heart of the tower to calculate the energy nodes keeping the heresy stable. Newton accepts the Law's consequences but secretly embedded the **"Equation of Grace"**—a mathematical backdoor in the tower's foundation.

### 🌹 Isabella Stuart: The Bitter Rose (The Usurper)
![[Isabella_after_art.png]]
Introduced as the sweet, resilient guide of the village. She is Newton's discarded variable. Her true goal is to betray the party, murder Hooke, steal the engine and (futurely) fuse her own blood with the Ascendant Engine to ascend as a goddess of chaos (escalating to a 15 Ultima Point Supreme Villain).

### 🔨 Helena: The Spark & Martyr
![[Helena_art.png]]
Though deceased, her "mechanical echoes" haunt the tower. If players collect her charred gloves and tools, they can use the visceral truth of her choice to break Hooke's psychological armor in the final fight.

## ⚙️ Custom Mechanics: The Dungeon's Core Loop
The Inverted Tower is an agonizing organism. Exploration follows a strict cycle every time the party enters a new room:

1.  **The Transition:** Gravity violently shifts upon entering. The *Labyrinthitis Clock* advances by 1 tick.
2.  **The Anomaly:** The GM rolls `1d6` to determine the room's physical rule (Lead Feet, Frictionless, Inverted Rain, Partial Vacuum, Zero Gravity, or Crushing).
3.  **The Resolution:** The party faces monsters or cinematic physics puzzles.
4.  **The Price:** Frivolous use of flight/teleportation magic or failing navigation tests advances the *Tower Gravitational Collapse Clock*.

### 🤢 Gravitational Sickness & Dramamine
The party must manage a global **6-Tick Labyrinthitis Clock**.
* **Stage 1 (3/6):** The party suffers the **Shaken** status.
* **Stage 2 (6/6):** Status worsens to **Slow**. In combat, characters must waste their Main Action to "purge" (vomit) or suffer direct Crisis damage to MP.
* **Dramamine Tincture:** Instantly clears the clock but inflicts the **Dazzled** status. An overdose forces the body into the **Sleep** status.

## ⚔️ The Final Confrontation: Relics, Grace & Betrayal

### 📜 Da Vinci's Manuscripts & The Engine
In the Broken Planetarium, Hooke commands the *Ascendant Engine*. If the players gathered the torn *Manuscripts of Da Vinci*, they learn its fatal flaw. Attacking its pressure valves causes massive fire damage to Hooke. However, this is also the moment **Isabella** strikes, attempting to fuse with the overheating core.

### 📐 Galileo's Calculations & The Equation of Grace
If the party rescues Isaac Newton or secures *Galileo's Calculations*, they unlock a theological and mechanical advantage. 
* **The Backdoor:** Any player can spend **1 Fabula Point** during the final confrontation to invoke the "Equation of Grace". This mathematical mercy instantly cancels a deadly room Anomaly for the remainder of the scene, proving that Grace can stabilize what Pride and Vengeance have broken.
