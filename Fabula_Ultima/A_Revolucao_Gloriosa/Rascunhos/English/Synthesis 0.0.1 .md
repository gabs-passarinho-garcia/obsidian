# 🍎 The Weight of Truth: The Inverted Tower

![[Torre_art.png]]

> *"Metal will yield. The weight will claim what is its own. The Law does not bend to our will, Robert."* — Isaac Newton

## 📜 Logline & Premise
A group of heroes arrives at Freefall Village, a place where the very fabric of reality is bleeding: sheep float aimlessly toward the clouds, and birds are brutally crushed against the cobblestones. The epicenter of this chaos is the **Inverted Tower**, a monument of black obsidian and twisted iron. To save the region, the party must "descend" up to the tower's peak to stop **Robert Hooke**, a broken architect attempting to destroy the laws of gravity to erase his own guilt over the death of the woman he loved.

---

## 📖 The Genesis of the Fall (Expanded Backstory)
Every tragedy needs a center of gravity, and in this story, it was Helena. She was a calloused, brilliant blacksmith who smelled of grease and soot, possessing a beauty that was a raw force of nature. She found forgotten manuscripts and divided them between the two men she loved: she gave the *Calculations of Galileo* to Isaac Newton (who sought to understand the quiet perfection of Creation) and the *Manuscripts of Da Vinci* to Robert Hooke (who sought to challenge the heavens).

The fracture began under an apple tree. As Helena rested her head on Newton's chest, an apple fell, striking him. Newton saw the undeniable Law of Gravity. Helena, laughing, bit into the apple. The sound of the skin cracking between her teeth marked the end of innocence. She chose the fruit of knowledge and the freedom of flight.

Hooke built the **Ascendant Engine**. Newton, having done the math, warned them the metal would yield. But fueled by pride, Hooke ignored the warning, and Helena chose to fly. The core overheated in the Howling Winds Gorge, melting the structure and tearing her skin before the brutal fall. Hooke's mind shattered. Refusing to bear the guilt, he blamed Gravity itself—the Creator's Law—for killing his love. He built the Inverted Tower to wage war on Creation.

## 🎭 The Fractured Triad (Core Characters)

### ⚙️ Robert Hooke: The Architect of Chaos
![[Hooke_art.png]]
Driven mad by guilt, Hooke is a physical and theological terrorist. He wears an aristocratic coat stained with grease and Helena's dried blood. He blames the "oppressive weight" of the universe for her death, rather than his own arrogant engineering.

### 🍎 Isaac Newton: The Prisoner of Law
![[Newton_art.png]]
Hooke's nemesis, imprisoned in the heart of the tower to calculate the energy nodes keeping the heresy stable. Newton doesn't hate Hooke; he pities him. Newton accepts the Law's consequences but secretly embedded the **"Equation of Grace"**—a mathematical backdoor in the tower's foundation that can stabilize the chaos if the heroes free him.

### 🔨 Helena: The Spark & Martyr
![[Helena_art.png]]
She chose to test the flying machine, and her death by its overheating core became the catalyst for the entire tragedy. Though deceased, her "mechanical echoes" haunt the tower. If players collect her charred gloves and tools, they can use the visceral truth of her choice to break Hooke's psychological armor in the final fight.

## ⚙️ Custom Mechanics: The Dungeon's Core Loop
The Inverted Tower is an agonizing organism. Exploration follows a strict cycle every time the party enters a new room:

1.  **The Transition:** Gravity violently shifts upon entering. The *Labyrinthitis Clock* advances by 1 tick.
2.  **The Anomaly:** The GM rolls `1d6` to determine the room's physical rule.
3.  **The Resolution:** The party faces monsters or cinematic physics puzzles.
4.  **The Price:** Frivolous use of flight/teleportation magic or failing navigation tests advances the *Tower Gravitational Collapse Clock*.

## 🤢 Gravitational Sickness: Labyrinthitis & Dramamine
The human body wasn't meant to fold with space. The party must manage a global **6-Tick Labyrinthitis Clock**.

* **Stage 1 - Cold Sweat (3/6 Ticks):** The party suffers the **Shaken** status.
* **Stage 2 - Gastric Collapse (6/6 Ticks):** The status worsens to **Slow**. In combat, characters must waste their Main Action to "purge" (vomit) or suffer direct Crisis damage to MP (Mind Points) if they ignore the nausea.

### 🧪 Inventory Item: Dramamine Tincture
A thick, bitter alchemical potion smelling of heavy-root and rust.
* **Cost/Action:** 2 IP (Inventory Points) and an Inventory Action to consume.
* **The Relief:** Instantly clears the Labyrinthitis Clock and cures sickness statuses.
* **The Side Effect:** The mind numbs. The user immediately suffers the **Dazzled** status due to severe drowsiness.
* **Overdose:** Taking a second dose while already *Dazzled* forces the body to shut down, inflicting the **Sleep/Unconscious** status for 1 full round.

## ⚔️ The Final Confrontation: Relics & Grace

### 📜 Da Vinci's Manuscripts & The Engine
![[Manuscritos_Da_Vinci_art.png]] ![[Motor_Ascendente_art.png]]
In the final room (The Broken Planetarium), Hooke commands the *Ascendant Engine*, a brutalist monstrosity of cast iron and violent purple energy. If the players gathered the torn, corrupted *Manuscripts of Da Vinci* throughout the dungeon, they learn the machine's fatal flaw. Attacking its pressure valves causes massive fire damage to Hooke, mirroring the exact overheating flaw that killed Helena.

### 📐 Galileo's Calculations & The Equation of Grace
![[Calculos_Galileu_art.png]]
If the party rescues Isaac Newton or secures *Galileo's Calculations* in the middle floors, they unlock a theological and mechanical advantage against Hooke. 
* **The Backdoor:** Any player can spend **1 Fabula Point** during the final confrontation to invoke the "Equation of Grace". This mathematical mercy instantly cancels a deadly room Anomaly for the remainder of the scene, proving that Grace can stabilize what Pride has broken.
